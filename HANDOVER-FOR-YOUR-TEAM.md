# Obsessed Minerals chatbot — guide for your team

This is a plain-language guide for the people who run Obsessed Minerals. No technical knowledge needed.

## What the chatbot does
- Answers visitor questions using only information that was checked and approved for Obsessed Minerals (chatbot version 5).
- If it doesn't know, it says so and points the visitor to a person instead of guessing.

## When a visitor needs a person
The chatbot notices when someone asks for a real person, or asks something it can't answer. Your chatbot builder gets an alert so someone can follow up. Please reply to those visitors as you normally would (phone, email or text).

## Changing an answer
Don't edit the chatbot files. Send the correct answer to your support contact below. They update it, test it, and send a new version.

## Turning it off
- Quick: ask your support contact, or have your web person remove the chat code from the website.
- Emergency: on the chatbot's hosting, set BOT_DISABLED=true and restart. Visitors see "Chat is unavailable right now".

## Saved conversations
Conversations are only saved if CHAT_LOG=file is set on the hosting. If you turn it on, mention it in your website's privacy policy.
